# Awesome AI Competitions [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of platforms, hackathons, academic challenges and learning resources for AI / machine learning / data science competitions.

Whether you want to sharpen your skills, win prize money, publish at a conference workshop, or just find your next hackathon, this list collects the places where AI competitions actually happen — plus the resources that help you do well in them.

## Featured Aggregators

*Sites that track competitions across many platforms, so you don't have to check each one.*

- [AI Competition Radar](https://www.aicompetition.dev/en) - Free aggregator of AI/ML competitions and hackathons from many platforms, with official links, favorites and a community forum.
- [ML Contests](https://mlcontests.com/) - List of ongoing machine learning competitions, plus yearly reports on the competition landscape.

## Competition Platforms

### Global

- [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions) - The largest data science competition platform, with featured, research, playground and community competitions.
- [DrivenData](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/) - Data science competitions focused on social impact.
- [AIcrowd](https://www.aicrowd.com/challenges) - Research-oriented challenges, many co-hosted with conferences such as NeurIPS.
- [Zindi](https://zindi.africa/competitions) - Data science competitions with a focus on challenges from Africa.
- [CodaLab Competitions](https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/) - Open-source platform widely used for academic benchmarks and challenges.
- [Codabench](https://www.codabench.org/) - Successor to CodaLab for hosting benchmarks and competitions.
- [EvalAI](https://eval.ai/) - Open-source platform for evaluating AI algorithms, popular for computer vision and multimodal challenges.
- [Hugging Face Competitions](https://huggingface.co/competitions) - Competitions hosted on the Hugging Face Hub.
- [Numerai](https://numer.ai/) - Ongoing data science tournament where participants build models on obfuscated financial data.
- [Bitgrit](https://bitgrit.com/competition/) - AI competitions with a global community of data scientists.
- [Topcoder](https://www.topcoder.com/challenges) - Crowdsourced challenges including data science and algorithm competitions.
- [CrowdANALYTIX](https://www.crowdanalytix.com/) - Crowdsourced analytics and machine learning contests.

### Regional

- [Tianchi 天池](https://tianchi.aliyun.com/competition/) - Alibaba Cloud's competition platform, one of the largest in China.
- [Heywhale 和鲸](https://www.heywhale.com/home/competition) - Chinese data science community and competition platform.
- [DataFountain](https://www.datafountain.cn/competitions) - Chinese data science competition and innovation platform.
- [Baidu AI Studio](https://aistudio.baidu.com/competition) - Competitions built around the PaddlePaddle ecosystem.
- [Biendata](https://www.biendata.xyz/) - Competition platform frequently used for academic and industry challenges in China.
- [SIGNATE](https://signate.jp/competitions) - Japan's largest data science competition platform.
- [Solafune](https://solafune.com/) - Japanese platform focused on satellite and geospatial data competitions.
- [Challenge Data (ENS)](https://challengedata.ens.fr/) - Data challenges from the École Normale Supérieure and Collège de France.

### Specialized and Open Innovation

- [ThinkOnward](https://thinkonward.com/) - Challenges in energy and geoscience data.
- [Unearthed](https://unearthed.solutions/) - Innovation challenges for the resources and energy industries.
- [HeroX](https://www.herox.com/crowdsourcing-projects) - Crowdsourced challenges and prize competitions, including AI topics.
- [XPRIZE](https://www.xprize.org/) - Large-scale incentive prize competitions, several centered on AI.

## Hackathon Platforms

- [Devpost](https://devpost.com/hackathons) - The largest directory of online and in-person hackathons.
- [Major League Hacking (MLH)](https://www.mlh.com/seasons/2026/events) - Official student hackathon league with events worldwide.
- [lablab.ai](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons) - AI hackathons built around the latest models and APIs.
- [DoraHacks](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon) - Hackathons and bounties, with a strong Web3 and AI community.
- [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/challenges/) - Hackathons and coding challenges, including ML tracks.
- [Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons) - Hackathons and competitions popular with students in India.
- [ETHGlobal](https://ethglobal.com/events) - Hackathons for Ethereum builders, often with AI agent tracks.

## Academic Challenges

- [NeurIPS Competition Track](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2024/CompetitionTrack) - Official competition track of the NeurIPS conference (see [neurips.cc](https://neurips.cc/) for the current year).
- [CVPR](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/) - CVPR workshops host many challenges each year, frequently run on Kaggle, EvalAI or CodaLab.
- [ICCV](https://iccv.thecvf.com/) - ICCV workshops also host a large number of computer vision challenges.
- [KDD Cup](https://www.kdd.org/kdd-cup) - Annual data mining competition of ACM SIGKDD, running since 1997.
- [Grand Challenge](https://grand-challenge.org/challenges/) - Biomedical image analysis challenges, many associated with MICCAI and ISBI.
- [ImageCLEF](https://www.imageclef.org/) - Evaluation campaign for multimodal and multilingual image retrieval and analysis.
- [AI City Challenge](https://www.aicitychallenge.org/) - Annual challenge on intelligent transportation, held with CVPR.
- [ChaLearn](https://www.chalearn.org/) - Organizer of machine learning challenges for academia since 2006.
- [RoboCup](https://robocup.org/) - International robotics and AI competition.

## Learning Resources and Past Solutions

- [The State of Machine Learning Competitions 2025](https://mlcontests.com/state-of-machine-learning-competitions-2025/) - Annual analysis of winning solutions, tools and trends ([2024 edition](https://mlcontests.com/state-of-machine-learning-competitions-2024/)).
- [Kaggle Solutions](https://github.com/faridrashidi/kaggle-solutions) - Collection of top Kaggle solutions and write-ups, also browsable at [kaggle.farid.one](https://kaggle.farid.one/).
- [Kaggle Past Solutions](https://github.com/EliotAndres/kaggle-past-solutions) - Searchable list of past Kaggle winning solutions.
- [Data Science Competitions](https://github.com/the-black-knight-01/Data-Science-Competitions) - Curated list of competition solutions and resources.
- [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn) - Free micro-courses on ML fundamentals from Kaggle.
- [Kaggle Code](https://www.kaggle.com/code) - Public notebooks, including many shared competition solutions.
- [Kaggle Discussions](https://www.kaggle.com/discussions) - Forums where winners often post detailed solution write-ups.
